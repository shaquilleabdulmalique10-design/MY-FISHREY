All About Fish

The biology, anatomy, and amazing facts you must know

Fish Anatomy

Gills for oxygen, fins for movement, lateral line system for sensing vibrations — fish are perfectly evolved for aquatic life.

Reproduction

Most fish lay eggs (spawning), but some give live birth. Breeding cycles depend on water temperature & seasons.

Osmoregulation

Freshwater and saltwater fish regulate water & salts differently — key knowledge for rearing success.

How to Rear Fish – Step-by-Step

From pond construction to harvesting, master the complete fish farming process.

1. Site Selection & Pond Prep

Choose area with good water source, soil quality, and drainage. Pond depth 1.5-2m, liming & fertilization essential.

2. Fingerling Stocking

Select healthy fingerlings, acclimatize to pond temperature. Stock density: 5000-8000 per acre depending on species.

3. Feeding & Nutrition

Balanced feed (protein 25-35%), feed 2-3 times daily. Floating pellets reduce waste. Monitor intake.

4. Water Quality Management

Maintain DO >4ppm, pH 6.5-8.5, ammonia low. Aeration & partial water changes critical.

5. Health & Disease Prevention

Quarantine new fish, use probiotics, avoid overcrowding. Common diseases: Ich, fungal infections.

6. Harvesting & Marketing

Partial or full harvest after 6-10 months. Use seine nets, transport in oxygenated tanks.

Tilapia

Fast growth, disease resistant, herbivorous/omnivorous. Ideal for warm freshwater.

African Catfish

High demand, air-breathing ability, high stocking density, very hardy.

Common Carp

Bottom feeder, adaptable, traditional pond fish, fast reproductive rate.

Rainbow Trout

Cold-water species, high oxygen requirement, excellent for RAS systems.

Fish Health & Common Diseases

Recognizing early signs of disease can save your stock. Keep water pristine, use medicated feed when necessary, and quarantine new arrivals.

  • White Spot (Ich) – treat with salt baths or formalin.
  • Fin Rot – improve water quality, antibacterial treatment.
  • Swim Bladder Disorder – caused by overfeeding, adjust diet.
  • Fungal Infections – cotton-like growth, use antifungal agents.
Pro Tip: Regular monitoring of fish behavior and water parameters prevents most diseases.

Vaccination & Biosecurity

Modern aquaculture uses vaccines against bacterial diseases. Maintain biosecurity, disinfect equipment, and record health logs.

Optimal parameters: Temp 25-30°C (tropical), Ammonia 0 ppm, Nitrite <0.1ppm

Feeding Tables

Fingerlings: 5-8% body weight/day. Growers: 2-4%. Adjust based on water temp & appetite.

Integrated Fish Farming

Combine fish with poultry or rice. Nutrient recycling, extra income, sustainable approach.

Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS)

Land-based, water reuse, high control. Suitable for urban farming and endangered species.
